   - Limitations and possible next steps.

     - dimensional consistency,
     - limiting cases (e.g., small/large parameter limits),

   - Explicitly discuss how the mathematical model was judged to be acceptable:
     - key modeling assumptions (e.g., rigid body, inviscid flow, quasi‑steady behavior),

     - internal consistency checks (e.g., the computed stress is less than the yield stress as a way to justify linear elasticity),

7. Tone and length
