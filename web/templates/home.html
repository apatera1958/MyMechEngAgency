Important Notes

In general, you should NOT Reset Session (and you should NOT clear your browser cookies). Your session will then be preserved so that you can return to your recent jobs. Use Reset Session only if specifically instructed to do so (by a site administrator) for purposes of troubleshooting.

Do not be impatient! Give "Queue job" a few seconds to respond (or you will end up with two identical jobs).

Transcript navigation is largely intuitive. Two pointers: clicking on a section button expands the section (it does not "jump" to the section); hovering over "Follow-On M" launches a pop-up with the full text of User Follow-On query M.

You may request an Engineering Notebook (Python Jupyter Notebook) on the Job page; you can then import and run the Notebook on (say) Google Colab.

You should use the Session Jobs page to check status of your jobs, and use the (particular) Job page to control execution (e.g., pose a Follow-On query, or Generate Engineering Notebook).

Submitted problems, optional hints, and generated results may be accessed by the site administrators for monitoring operation or troubleshooting, for evaluating the performance of MyMechEngAgency, and for understanding the breadth of engineering problems to which the system can be applied. Jobs are associated with a browser session rather than a named user and are normally retained for 96 hours. An IP address associated with a job is recorded automatically for rate-limiting purposes and in principle can be retrieved by the site administrators; however, the site administrators will NOT access or use IP-address information to identify users. Jobs will therefore be treated as anonymous by the site administrators unless a user chooses to include identifying information in the Problem Statement or other user-submitted material.

Site maintenance (re-deployments) will be performed 5am-6am the first Tuesday of each month; please do not launch sessions during the site maintenance period, and please download all your job results before the start of the site maintenance period.
